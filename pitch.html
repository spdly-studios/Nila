THE GAP

Attendance tells you who is missing.
It rarely tells you why.

For years, schools have recorded absence as a binary mark.

  • Reasons disappear into phone calls and scattered notes.
  • Patterns across weeks, students, and weekdays stay hidden.
  • Teachers are left to chase context manually.
